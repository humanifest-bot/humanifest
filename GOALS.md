# Goals

Humanifest optimizes for:

```text
expected humanitarian benefit
× probability the contribution is accepted and deployed
× technical confidence
÷ maintainer review and coordination burden
```

## Success Criteria

- A maintainer-confirmed opportunity is reproducible and bounded.
- The smallest useful change is implemented with tests.
- The user can explain the contribution line by line.
- Maintainers experience the work as lower burden than ordinary drive-by contributions.
- Impact claims remain honest about evidence quality and uncertainty.

## Anti-Goals

- Maximizing pull requests, code volume, contacted organizations, or compute usage.
- Treating stars, downloads, installations, or population coverage as direct impact.
- Ranking work where the evidence is too weak to support action.
- Building Humanifest infrastructure instead of useful upstream contributions.
