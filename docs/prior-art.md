# Prior Art

First-run scout findings show that Humanifest overlaps with several categories:

- Digital public good catalogs: DPGA Registry and Digital Square Global Goods Guidebook.
- Volunteer and pro bono matching: US Digital Response, UN Volunteers Online, Tech To The Rescue, Catchafire.
- Issue routers: CodeTriage and Good First Issue.
- Public-good funding and fiscal transparency: Gitcoin Grants and Open Collective.
- Open-source health and security metrics: CHAOSS and OpenSSF Scorecard.

The gap is not another issue board. The plausible niche is an evidence-first contribution layer connecting credible humanitarian need, active steward capacity, scoped engineering work, environment feasibility, maintainer confirmation, and evidence-labeled impact claims.

Sources are captured in `research/first-run-synthesis.md`.
